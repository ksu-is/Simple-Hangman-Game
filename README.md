## How to Play Hangman
Player will be prompted with three difficulty settings: easy, medium, and hard. They will enter what difficulty they prefer. Next, the player will attempt to guess the word based on the empty word spaces. Each incorrect word will add a body part of the Hangman to the code, and upon six incorrect letter guesses, the player will fail. If the player manages to correctly guess the hidden word, they will win.
